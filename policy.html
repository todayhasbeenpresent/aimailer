1. Disclaimer

AI Mailer Pro is software for creating, organizing and sending email campaigns through supported sending infrastructure. We do not guarantee inbox placement, delivery rates, sender reputation, uninterrupted access, or acceptance by any mailbox provider.

Use of the software does not grant permission to send a message to any recipient. The customer is responsible for the legality, accuracy and appropriateness of campaigns, recipient lists, content, domains and sending infrastructure.

2. Terms of Use

By purchasing, installing or using AI Mailer Pro, you agree to use the software lawfully and in accordance with these policies, applicable laws, and the rules of any connected provider.

  • Keep account, license and payment information accurate.
  • Do not reverse engineer, crack, bypass or defeat licensing or security controls.
  • Do not resell, sublicense or redistribute the software unless your plan explicitly permits it.
  • Maintain control of your connected SMTP/API credentials and use them only as authorized.

3. Acceptable Use Policy

AI Mailer Pro must not be used to facilitate fraud, phishing, impersonation, malware distribution, credential theft, deceptive schemes, unlawful harassment, or other illegal activity.

Customers must not use the product to evade provider enforcement, defeat spam filters, conceal sender identity, rotate accounts to bypass limits, or continue sending after a provider has prohibited the activity.

Customers must have an appropriate lawful basis or other permission required for their communications. Where marketing laws require consent, prior relationship, opt-out handling, or other controls, the customer must implement them.

Commercial email: In the United States, CAN-SPAM requirements include accurate header information, non-deceptive subject lines, identification of commercial messages, a valid physical postal address, a working opt-out mechanism, and prompt honoring of opt-outs. The FTC also makes clear that using another company to send email does not automatically remove the marketer's responsibility. FTC guidance.

4. Privacy

We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate the website, process purchases, provide support, manage licenses, and improve the service. Depending on the service flow, this may include contact information, license/account information, payment-related records, support communications and technical information.

We do not claim ownership of customer campaign content merely because the customer uses our software. Customers remain responsible for ensuring that the personal data they import, process or send is collected and used lawfully.

Where privacy laws apply to a customer's campaign, the customer is responsible for determining its role as controller/business and for providing any required notices, consents, rights handling and contractual protections.

5. Data Storage

AI Mailer Pro may operate as local software and may interact with third-party services selected by the customer. Data stored locally by the application is subject to the customer's device, operating system, backups and security practices.

Where our website or licensing infrastructure receives information, we retain it only for legitimate operational, security, support, accounting or legal purposes and according to the applicable privacy notice. We do not promise that every third-party service used by a customer stores data in a particular country or region.

Customer responsibility

  • Use appropriate device security and backups.
  • Do not place unnecessary sensitive personal information into campaign files.
  • Protect exported lists, credentials, templates and campaign records.
  • Delete data when it is no longer needed for the customer's purpose or legal obligations.

6. SMTP & API Limitation Policy

AI Mailer Pro is not the customer's SMTP provider, Gmail/Google Workspace account, mailbox provider, domain host, or internet service provider. SMTP and API functionality is dependent on the connected provider's current authentication methods, quotas, rate limits, permissions and policies.

  • Provider quotas and limits can change without notice.
  • Authentication failures, revoked tokens, rate limits, account restrictions and provider outages may prevent sending.
  • We cannot guarantee that a provider will approve or continue to support a particular sending pattern.
  • Customers must comply with the connected provider's terms and acceptable-use rules.
  • Customers must not use multiple accounts or credentials for the purpose of evading provider enforcement.

For Gmail API integrations, Google publishes API quotas and usage limits that can restrict request rates. See Google's current Gmail API quota documentation.

7. Email, Deliverability & Recipient Policy

AI Mailer Pro does not guarantee that messages will reach the inbox. Delivery can be affected by sender reputation, authentication, recipient engagement, content, domain configuration, provider policies, throttling, bounces and complaints.

Customers should maintain accurate lists, honor suppression/opt-out requests, authenticate sending domains where supported, monitor bounces and complaints, and avoid deceptive sender information or subject lines.

For marketing email, customers should provide legally required identification and unsubscribe mechanisms. Provider-specific requirements may be stricter than general law.

8. Security & Credentials

Customers are responsible for protecting SMTP passwords, API keys, OAuth tokens, license credentials and devices on which AI Mailer Pro is installed. Do not share credentials with people who are not authorized to use the connected account.

If you believe credentials have been exposed, revoke or rotate them through the relevant provider immediately and contact us if the incident involves our systems.

9. Third-Party Services

AI Mailer Pro may integrate with or rely on services operated by third parties, including email providers, payment processors, hosting providers and APIs. Those services have their own terms, privacy policies, technical limitations and availability.

We are not responsible for changes, outages, suspensions or policy decisions made by an independent third-party provider. A provider may restrict an account even when the software itself is functioning normally.

10. Content, Intellectual Property & Abuse

Customers must have the rights or permissions necessary to use the text, images, trademarks, attachments, databases and other materials they send. Do not use AI Mailer Pro to distribute material that infringes another person's intellectual property or rights.

We may investigate credible abuse reports and may restrict access where necessary to protect the service, other users, providers, recipients or legal interests.

11. Billing, Licensing & Refunds

Paid access is licensed, not a transfer of ownership of the software. Pricing, plan duration, renewal terms and any refund eligibility should be shown at checkout or in the applicable order terms.

A provider suspension, deliverability issue, campaign result, or inability to use a third-party SMTP/API account does not automatically mean that the software itself failed. Any refund request is reviewed according to the applicable purchase terms.

12. Suspension, Termination & Abuse Response

We may suspend or terminate access where we reasonably believe an account is being used for illegal activity, abuse, credential theft, fraud, malware, deceptive practices, licensing violations, provider-evasion, or material violations of these policies.

Where appropriate, we may request information needed to investigate abuse. We may also cooperate with lawful requests from authorities or service providers.

13. Policy Changes

We may update these policies as the product, third-party providers, laws or industry requirements change. The latest version published on this page will govern future use. Material changes may be highlighted on the website or communicated through available account channels.

14. Contact & Abuse Reports

For support, policy questions, suspected abuse, or requests concerning your account, contact the AI Mailer Pro support address shown on the main website or your purchase/support documentation.

Report abuse: If you receive a message that appears to misuse AI Mailer Pro, preserve the message and relevant headers if possible and contact us with enough information to identify the campaign or sender. Do not send passwords, API keys or other secrets in an abuse report.
